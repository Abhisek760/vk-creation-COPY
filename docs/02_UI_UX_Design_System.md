34. Premium UX Features (Must Have)
Smart Sticky Booking Button

As the user scrolls, a floating "Book Your Event" button appears so booking is always one tap away.

Before & After Slider

For selected photos, users can drag a slider to compare the original shot and the edited version. This showcases your editing quality.

Live Package Price Calculator

In Build Your Coverage, the estimated budget updates instantly as services are selected.

Event Countdown

After booking, the client dashboard displays:

"Your wedding is in 48 days."

Exit Intent Popup (Desktop)

If a visitor is about to leave without booking, show a subtle popup:

"Planning your special day? Get a custom quote in just 2 minutes."

(No aggressive marketing.)
